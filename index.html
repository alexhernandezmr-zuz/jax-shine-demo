About us

Jacksonville born, detail obsessed

JAX Shine started with one simple idea: a car wash should never be rushed. We work on every vehicle like it is our own, using quality products that are safe for your paint.

Between the sun, the salt air and the lovebugs, Florida is tough on cars. We know how to protect them, and we come to you with our mobile setup, or you can drop off at our shop.
